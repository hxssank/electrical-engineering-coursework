# CEN 800 | Law and Ethics in Engineering Practice

The course examines professional responsibility alongside Canadian legal institutions, contracts, liability, intellectual property, workplace safety, sustainability, and dispute resolution.

## A design-review dilemma

In a hypothetical review, a prototype exceeds its stated temperature limit, but a delivery deadline is approaching. The decision record should identify the measured result, the unresolved risk, the affected parties, and who has authority to accept or reject the change.

A useful comparison separates three questions: what the agreement requires, what the available evidence supports, and what protects people affected by the design. Passing a contractual milestone does not establish that an unresolved technical hazard is acceptable.

This is an engineering ethics study example, not a legal determination about a real project.
