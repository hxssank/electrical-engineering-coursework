# CHY 102 | General Chemistry

Engineering chemistry covers reaction quantities, states of matter, equilibrium, energy changes, reaction rates, nuclear processes, and electrochemistry. Calculations connect the chemical model to measurable quantities and units.

## Ideal-gas example

For an illustrative sample with 1.00 mol of gas at 298 K in 0.0240 m³, the ideal-gas relation gives:

```text
P = nRT / V
  = 1.00 × 8.314 × 298 / 0.0240
  = 103,232 Pa, approximately 103 kPa
```

The temperature must be absolute, and the volume must match the units used for the gas constant. The result depends on the ideal-gas approximation. A real-gas model may be needed when intermolecular effects or molecular volume matter.
