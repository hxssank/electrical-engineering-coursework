# CMN 432 | Communication in the Engineering Professions

Engineering communication requires an explanation that fits its audience and gives the reader enough evidence to act. The course develops analysis, writing, presentations, and problem solving through professional communication tasks.

## Turning an observation into a decision

Compare two hypothetical report sentences:

- "The cable performs poorly."
- "At 2 A, the measured cable drop was 0.40 V, above the project's 0.25 V limit. Recheck the connector resistance before changing the conductor size."

The second sentence identifies the operating condition, the observation, the acceptance criterion, and the next test. It also separates the measured symptom from a cause that has not yet been established.

A technical report should make that distinction visible in its tables, figures, and recommendations.
