# ECN 440 | Economic Issues in Financial Markets

The course examines recurring speculative booms, financial instability, and market collapses through historical cases, including early asset bubbles, the 1929 crash, and later technology and credit crises.

## Leverage changes the loss

Consider a hypothetical asset purchase funded with $100 of equity and $400 of debt. The asset initially costs $500. A 10% decline reduces its value to $450. If the debt remains $400, the remaining equity is $50.

The asset lost 10%, while the equity lost 50%. This simplified example ignores interest, transaction costs, margin requirements, and taxes. It illustrates how borrowing can magnify a change in asset value into a much larger change in the owner's stake. It is not a trading recommendation.
