# MTL 200 | Materials Science Fundamentals

Materials science connects atomic bonding and structure to engineering behavior. Topics include crystal structures, defects, diffusion, phase changes, corrosion, and electrical and thermal properties of metals, polymers, and ceramics.

## Thermal-expansion example

Assume a uniform part has initial length 0.10 m and a constant expansion coefficient of `12 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹`. For a temperature increase of 50 K:

```text
ΔL = α L₀ ΔT
   = 12 × 10⁻⁶ × 0.10 × 50
   = 60 micrometres
```

This estimate assumes free expansion. A constrained part can develop stress, and bonded materials can expand by different amounts. Those distinctions matter when checking dimensional clearances and connections across a temperature range.
