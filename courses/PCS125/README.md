# PCS 125 | Physics: Waves and Fields

This course connects oscillation and wave motion with gravitational, electric, and magnetic fields. It covers interference, standing waves, resonance, and relationships between fields and potential energy.

## Frequency and wavelength

For an illustrative travelling wave with frequency 2 Hz and wavelength 0.50 m:

```text
wave speed = frequency × wavelength
           = 2 × 0.50
           = 1.0 m/s
```

Frequency describes cycles per second; wavelength describes the distance between equal-phase points. Amplitude describes displacement or field magnitude and does not replace either quantity in this relation.

When waves overlap, their phase difference affects the combined amplitude. A standing-wave pattern therefore requires attention to boundary conditions and interference, not just the frequency of the source.
