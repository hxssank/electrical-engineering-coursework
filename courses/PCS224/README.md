# PCS 224 | Solid State Physics

Solid-state physics connects quantum behavior and energy bands to material properties and electronic devices. The course studies conduction in metals and semiconductors, junctions, transistors, and related devices.

## Carrier density and conductivity

In a simplified single-carrier model, conductivity is:

```text
σ = q n μ
```

Here `q` is charge magnitude, `n` is carrier density, and `μ` is mobility. With illustrative values `q = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C`, `n = 10²¹ m⁻³`, and `μ = 0.10 m²/(V·s)`, the conductivity is `16.02 S/m`.

The model separates the number of available carriers from how readily they move. Materials with both electron and hole contributions require both terms, and mobility can depend on temperature and scattering mechanisms.
